# D04: learner quiz answer positioning

The original six-case live content report remains unchanged. Its five-question quiz had correct index0 for all questions and a positional explanation in question5 (“lựa chọn đầu tiên”). The independent [semantic review](reports/v7-live-content-review.json) remains the review of that original run, including duplicate-meaning and conflict-context defects.

After validating every original question, `validateStructured` now calls the pure `balanceQuizOptions` helper. It uses a stable content-derived tie breaker and places eligible correct answers in the least-used positions by circularly rotating the existing four options. Exact option strings, correct-answer text, question identity, references, explanation, topic and difficulty are preserved. Input objects are not mutated; repeating normalization is idempotent. When all questions are eligible, position counts differ by at most1. This is deterministic presentation balancing, **not randomness or a secure-exam shuffle**. Existing saved quizzes are not rewritten, and the teacher generation workflow is unchanged.

Explicit positional dependencies in prompts, options or explanations keep their original order. Bounded cues cover option letters/numbers/ordinals, labelled choices and common “all of the above”/combined-letter forms in the five supported languages. Mathematical variables such as `A=8w` are not treated as generic letter references. Preserved cases carry the `positional-options-preserved` quality issue and a specific localized review notice; they are not rejected into another paid attempt. Fixed positions can make an overall batch unbalanced.

This cue detector is conservative, not complete natural-language understanding. Rare phrasing may require human review; false-positive preservation is preferable to making a position-dependent explanation false. It does not repair semantic duplicate questions, validate mathematical truth, change source claims or replace teacher review. The general result remains `review-required`.
